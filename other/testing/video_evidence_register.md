# Video evidence register

Only YouTube links are committed. Raw `.MOV` files and locally generated
explained `.mp4` files are intentionally ignored and remain outside the Git
history.

## Open Challenge

[Watch the Open Challenge demonstration](https://youtu.be/8kb7vfMW33Q?si=mpr8wsterBccIcVb)

Team record for the linked historical demonstration:

- direction: clockwise;
- completes three laps;
- stops autonomously; and
- contains no manual intervention after starting.

This is useful evidence that the Open Challenge concept operated successfully.
It predates the November 21 Steering Knuckles V2 revision and has no matching
test-log configuration sheet, so it is not counted as one of the three required
final-configuration regression trials.

## Obstacle Challenge

[Watch the Obstacle Challenge demonstration](https://youtu.be/Gv9RtIQvm2Q?si=-njMhDYktdj0zBOS)

Team record for the linked historical demonstration:

- direction: counter-clockwise;
- shows parking exit and parking entry;
- shows full-lap operation, but the video does not contain all three complete
  laps; and
- contains no manual intervention after starting.

The linked video demonstrates the map-driven obstacle concept and parking
sequence. It does not establish a complete three-lap result, final parking
repeatability or final-configuration regression.

## Final evidence requirement

For a future YouTube upload to count as a formal trial, it needs a matching
test log that identifies:

1. final robot mechanical and electrical configuration;
2. controller, firmware and JSON/configuration versions;
3. direction and complete field/pillar layout;
4. start and autonomous stop conditions;
5. contacts, interventions, faults and parking result; and
6. completion time and the continuous-video link.

An edited explanation can help judges understand the design, but the formal
result should be supported by continuous footage and its matching log.
